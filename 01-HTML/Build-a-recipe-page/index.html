Quest Nacho Upgrade Plate

The Vibe: Crunchy, cheesy, stadium-style nachos completely reimagined as a low-carb powerhouse. Balanced with a crisp, fiery hit of fresh, homemade pico de gallo and tangy Greek yogurt to cut through the rich cheddar.

Ingredients

  • Ground Chicken 1/2lb
  • Quest Nacho Cheese Protein Chips 2 bags
  • Sharp Shredded Cheddar Cheese 1/2 cup
  • Flavor Bomb Cherry Tomatoes 4oz
  • Red Onion 1/2
  • Jalapeño 1
  • Lime 1
  • Non-fat greek Yogurt 2 Tbsp
  • Taco Seasoning 1/2 packet
  • Salt and Pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Cook the Meat: Brown 8 oz of extra-lean ground chicken in a skillet over medium-high heat. Season heavily with your taco seasoning or Southwest spice blend. Cook for 5–7 minutes until completely done and set aside.
  2. Mix the Pico: While the meat cooks, finely dice the Flavor Bomb cherry tomatoes, half red onion, and deseeded jalapeño. Toss together in a small bowl with chopped fresh cilantro, a pinch of salt, and the juice of a fresh lime. Set aside to let flavors marry.
  3. Layer and Melt: Lay out 2 bags of Quest Nacho Cheese Protein Chips evenly across two oven-safe plates or a sheet pan. Pile the hot seasoned ground chicken directly onto the chips. Top generously with the shredded sharp cheddar cheese and pop under the broiler for 60 seconds until bubbling and melted.
  4. Top and Serve: Finish each plate with a fresh dollop of plain non-fat Greek yogurt, 1 tbsp each, and your fresh homemade Pico de Gallo. Serve hot!
