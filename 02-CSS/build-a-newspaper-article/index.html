Roaming Bear Steals Bird Feeders
Pre-winter bulking season hits peak absurdity as neighborhood black bear elevates petty theft to an art form.
By Mark Basile

In what local law enforcement is calling the most polite crime spree of the decade, a black bear preparing for winter sleep has bypassed berry bushes entirely to focus on high-calorie backyard fixtures. The culprit has shown a particular culinary preference for suet cakes and organic nyjer seed, completely ignoring cheap store-brand mixes and leaving behind polite, albeit massive, paw prints on porch steps.

"He's not even breaking the plastic feeders anymore," said resident Dave Higgins, who caught the bear on his doorbell camera casually carrying off a tube feeder like a giant string cheese. "He just unscrews the top, dumps it into his mouth, and looks directly at the lens. The guy knows the park rangers can't arrest him before November."
