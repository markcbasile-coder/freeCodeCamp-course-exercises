Derwyn the Defender

A warrior's path through the chaos of the Sword Coast

Timeline:

  • 1 Eleint, 1492 DR - Begins his journey by recovering a mysterious puzzle box from a dimly lit basement, immediately sensing an unsettling dark magic attached to it.
  • 3 Eleint, 1492 DR - Helps ambush a goblin raiding party on the Triboar Trail and tracks the survivors back to their hideout to rescue captured allies.
  • 4 Eleint, 1492 DR - Cleaves through goblin defenders in the Cragmaw caves and delivers the blow that brings down the bugbear leader, Klarg.
  • 6 Eleint, 1492 DR - Pays his respects at the Shrine of Luck in Phandalin before heading out to clear the surrounding wilderness of regional threats.
  • 8 Eleint, 1492 DR - Holds the line at Wyvern Tor, badly wounding a massive ogre at a narrow choke point to clear the way for his party.
  • 9 Eleint, 1492 DR - Leaves a copper piece on the grave of a fallen comrade in a silent gesture of honor after clearing out the remaining mountain threats.
  • 10 Eleint, 1492 DR - Explores the subterranean ruins beneath Tresendar Manor, braving a terrifying Nothic to claim the legendary ancient greatsword, Talon.
  • 11 Eleint, 1492 DR - Breaches the hidden workshop of GlassStaff, putting a swift end to the traitorous wizard's reign over the town.
  • 13 Eleint, 1492 DR - Drives off a cult of dragon worshippers in the ruins of Thundertree before facing the green dragon Venomfang.
  • 14 Eleint, 1492 DR - Strikes a blow against the bronze dragon Zilshokamoc, shattering its armor to protect his party in a deadly confrontation.
  • 16 Eleint, 1492 DR - Advances into the depths of Wave Echo Cave, standing ready to face the Black Spider and protect his bound companions.
"Derwyn's deeds and unwavering resolve are testimony to the far-reaching impact that one warrior's fierce courage, loyalty, and steady blade can bring to the safety of the Sword Coast."

— Sildar Hallwinter, Lord's Alliance
